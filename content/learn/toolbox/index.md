# Toolbox

The building blocks you draw in a high-level design, grouped by the job they
do. Each page compares the usual options, so you can say why you picked one.

- [Databases](databases.md): relational, key-value and wide-column stores, and how to choose between them.
- [In-memory stores](in-memory-stores.md): fast reads, counters, locks and leaderboards held in memory.
- [Queues and logs](queues-and-logs.md): handing work between services, and when you need a replayable log.
- [Search engines](search-engines.md): text search, filters and ranking over large collections.
- [Object storage and CDNs](object-storage-and-cdns.md): keeping large files cheaply and serving them close to users.
- [Stream processing](stream-processing.md): counting, joining and aggregating events as they arrive.
- [Coordination services](coordination-services.md): leader election, locks and shared configuration.
- [Load balancers and gateways](load-balancers-and-gateways.md): spreading traffic, and handling auth and limits at the edge.
